security policy

report vulnerabilities privately at security@yourproject.com or through the "report a vulnerability" button in the security tab. do not open public issues.

include a description, steps to reproduce, affected versions, and any workarounds.

we will acknowledge within 3 business days and keep you updated until it is fixed. accepted reports are credited unless you prefer otherwise. declined reports will include an explanation.

please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.
